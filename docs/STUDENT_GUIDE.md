# Student guide

Use the account created by the administrator. Local accounts must change the initial password at first sign-in. For an approved Microsoft account, choose school Microsoft sign-in; CLASO updates the full name from that identity. There is no automatic signup. Your administrator or school management enrolls you in classes; an empty workspace means no classes have been assigned yet. Today shows your enrolled classes, timetable, and published work. The language switcher is always available; Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Academic passport collects your class relationships and released results.

## Assignments

Read the instructions and due time before submitting. You can attach a PDF, supported image, or plain-text file. Your submission is final and is stored once even if a request is retried. Late submission is accepted only when the teacher enabled it. A teacher may grade privately first; your score and feedback appear when returned.

## Exams

Open an available exam and start or resume an attempt. Question order may be randomized. Use numbered navigation to move between questions. The saved-state indicator reports when the server accepted answers. The timer is based on the server's deadline, including after refresh.

Keep the page open during a connection problem. Unsent answers remain in that tab's memory and retry after reconnection, while the attempt is open. Reloading or closing the tab can discard unsaved input. The deadline continues during an outage. Report an outage to your teacher; the application does not claim offline exam submission.

Final submission asks for confirmation and prevents further edits. Results appear only after the teacher releases them. You cannot open another student's attempts or results, or discover drafts through search.

## Help

Inbox supports messages to your assigned teachers and administrators. Contact an administrator for account recovery. Microsoft passwords are managed by school IT. Local passwords can be changed through the profile control or reset by the system administrator. Sign out on shared devices. Do not share passwords or leave a school session open on a public computer.
