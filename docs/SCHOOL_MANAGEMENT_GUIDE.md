# School management

Sign in with the account supplied by the system administrator and change its initial password. The School management workspace provides a control center for academic operations across the whole school.

- Academic structure: years, terms, grades, sections, tracks, subjects and classes; teacher assignments and student enrollment.
- Teaching and assessments: lessons, learning resources, assignments, grading and returned results; question banks and the full exam publication lifecycle.
- School operations: schedules with conflict checks, targeted announcements, academic reports and historical years.
- Follow-up: actual active-year counts for classes lacking teachers/students, pending grading and closed exams awaiting results. The class list shows at most 25 gaps and links to the class workspace.
- People: read the student/teacher directory. Use Academic structure to manage their memberships.
- Activity: read academic audit events. Account and security events are reserved for the administrator.

The administrator alone creates accounts, changes roles, resets credentials, archives/restores identities, imports/exports accounts and manages school/system settings. Contact that person when an account is missing or access needs changing. Microsoft and local identity approvals are not delegated to school management.

Archived academic work stays read-only for all roles. Finishing a year requires completing and releasing received results; account changes never erase academic history.

The setup checklist is visible in Control center and, while incomplete, on the dashboard. Identity and account creation steps are labeled for the administrator and have no management edit action. Academic setup links remain usable. Coverage applies to every active class in the active academic year; archived records cannot make incomplete current setup appear complete.
